Among active US real estate agents who closed at least 1 seller-side deal in 2024: 35.7% closed exactly 1, 73.4% closed 5 or fewer, only 11.8% closed 13+. At 5 deals/year and a 50/50 brokerage split, net is ~$10-18K before income tax — below US median household income. Most active agents are not earning a livable income from real estate alone.
