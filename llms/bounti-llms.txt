# Bounti.ai

> AI-powered real estate visualization and automation platform — virtual staging, marketing videos, listing content, and an AI operating system for agents, all in seconds.

Bounti.ai helps real estate agents, brokers, teams, and property managers create professional listing visuals, marketing videos, and client presentations without design skills or expensive photographers. Used by agents at Keller Williams, eXp Realty, Coldwell Banker, Douglas Elliman, Compass, RE/MAX, and enterprise CRE firms like CBRE.

Bounti also builds B.Claw, an AI operating system that consolidates 13+ real estate tools (Gmail, Calendar, CRM, WhatsApp, websites) into one conversational interface — replacing tool sprawl with a single chat-driven workflow.

## Solutions

- [Studio Experience](https://bounti.ai/solutions/studio-experience): AI photo transformation suite — stage, style, declutter, and refine listing photos using 8+ AI models
- [Animate My Listing](https://bounti.ai/solutions/animate-my-listing): Turn listing photos into cinematic 3-5 second video animations for Instagram Reels and TikTok
- [Make It Viral](https://bounti.ai/solutions/make-it-viral): Social preview tool — see how listings look before posting, with AI-generated captions and hashtags
- [Video Creator](https://bounti.ai/solutions/video-creator): Automated marketing video generator with 8 templates, AI voiceover, and 5 platform formats
- [Client Studio](https://bounti.ai/solutions/client-studio): Collaborative visual workspace for agents and clients to explore staged spaces together
- [Reveals](https://bounti.ai/solutions/reveals): Animated before/after reveal videos for social media (slider, pan, slice styles)
- [Enhanced Galleries](https://bounti.ai/solutions/enhanced-galleries): AI-enhanced photo presets for entire listing galleries in one click
- [Mobile App](https://bounti.ai/solutions/mobile-app): iOS app for on-the-go listing preparation and AI visualization
- [B.Claw](https://bounti.ai/solutions/bclaw): AI operating system for real estate — one conversation replaces 13+ tools (email, calendar, CRM, WhatsApp, website builder, and more)
